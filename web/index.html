Three engines that lock to your tempo.

Not "roughly in time". They take their phase from the transport, so the first step of the pattern is the first beat of your bar — whenever you hit play.

Arpeggiator

Hold a chord, pick Up / Down / Up‑Down / Random. 1/4 down to 1/32 with triplets, one to three octaves, adjustable gate. Every note keeps the velocity of the key that fed it.

Sidechain pump

The duck every dance record is built on, with nothing to route. One dial, four rates: a bar, a half, a quarter, an eighth. Anchored to the bar line, not to whenever the plugin loaded.

Glide

Portamento on a dial. Notes slide in from the one before them — the 808 slide, the acid line, the dub siren. Drum kit pieces are left alone, because sliding between drums is never what you meant.

Signal path

One window, front to back.

01 / IN

Load anything, or load nothing

Drop a vocal, a break, a field recording. Or skip it and play the synth — the keys are never silent, even on a fresh instance.

02 / SLICE

Chop by transient or by grid

Detect hits automatically with a sensitivity dial, or cut to 4, 8, 16 or 32 equal slices. The pieces land across the keyboard in the order they were cut, so a run up the keys is a run through the sample.

03 / ENGINE

Four ways to play it

Chop triggers your slices. Synth plays 403 built-in voices. Sampled loads your own SFZ multisample banks. Melody plays the loaded sample chromatically across the keys. Switch mid-performance; nothing clicks.

04 / SHAPE

A real synth underneath

Seven-voice unison with spread and drift, sub and noise, FM, vibrato, a resonant filter with its own envelope and LFO, chorus, saturation, and a vowel formant bank that makes the vocal patches read as a voice. Three macros — HYPE, SPACE, DIRT — move the lot at once.

05 / OUT

Pitch, texture, FX, limiter

Formant-preserving pitch shift, granular texture, drive, reverb and a tempo-locked delay, into a look-ahead brick-wall limiter. True bypass on the pitch engine, so ordinary playing never pays for latency it isn't using.

Loop station

Build the whole beat without leaving the plugin.

Six tracks, each with its own pre-picked sound, so a tap on REC drops you straight into the right instrument. Record, overdub, undo the last pass, reverse a track, pan it, mute it. Track one sets the length and everything after quantises to it.

Follow the host tempo or tap your own. Export the mix as a WAV — or hold DRAG and drop it straight onto your DAW's timeline.
